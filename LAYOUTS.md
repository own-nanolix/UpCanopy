# LAYOUTS: one line per page, one line per mockup

Direction for this project (from `rule/08-upcanopy.md`, section 2b):

- **Mockup staging:** diagonal cascade: objects stepping across the frame and cropping off its edges.
- **Mockup backdrop:** a seamless studio sweep tinted with the lightest brand colour (Green Ligth `#F2FFE4`).
- **Brand pages lean on:** a split screen: a fixed rail on one side, scrolling content on the other.
- **Dashboard option A is:** a light editorial layout led by large figures; B to E are four other structures.
- **Landing pages lean on:** modular cards that reflow, led by product UI.

Rules: where the case study shows a layout, the case study wins. Nothing repeats: no two lines below may be
built the same way. Builders follow their lines; reviewers check against them.
